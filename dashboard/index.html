Why BTS Marketing Carrier data is required

PAE beats SEA on 4 of 8 routes (PHX, PDX, LAX, LAS), but SFO makes up nearly 1 in 5 PAE flights and drags PAE's headline rate below SEA.

BTS splits delays into causes only for flights arriving 15+ minutes late (on-time and early flights count as 0). Each slice shows average 15+ minute delay minutes per completed flight from that cause. Outbound PAE → SFO airspace holds turn directly into inbound SFO → PAE late-arriving-aircraft delays when the same Horizon jet turns around.

Excluding SFO, Paine Field beat Sea-Tac in 11 of 12 months. During SFO's spring and summer marine-layer season (Apr–Aug), that single route pulls Paine Field's overall on-time rate down by up to ~13 percentage points.

Data source: U.S. DOT Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) Marketing Carrier On-Time Performance, Oct 2025 – Sep 2026 (data lags ~2 months).

Definitions: A flight is on time if it arrives less than 15 minutes late. Destinations reflect active PAE commercial routes (LAS, LAX, PDX, PHX, PSP seasonal, SAN, SFO, SNA).
