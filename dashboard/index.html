Why BTS Marketing Carrier data is required

PAE beats SEA on 4 of 8 routes (PHX, PDX, LAX, LAS), while SFO makes up 19.3% of PAE's schedule and drags PAE's headline rate below SEA.

BTS splits delays into causes only for flights arriving 15+ minutes late (on-time and early flights count as 0). Each slice shows average 15+ minute delay minutes per completed flight from that cause. Outbound PAE → SFO airspace holds turn directly into inbound SFO → PAE late-arriving-aircraft delays when the same Horizon jet turns around.

Excluding SFO, Paine Field beat Sea-Tac in 11 of 12 months. At its peak, the SFO route alone pulls Paine Field's monthly on-time rate down by 13.3 percentage points.

Historical data: U.S. DOT Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) Marketing Carrier On-Time Performance, Oct 2025 – Sep 2026 (auto-checked weekly; DOT publishes on a ~2-month lag; built Nov 23, 2026). A flight is on time if it arrives less than 15 minutes late.
